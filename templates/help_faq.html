❓ Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions or ask our AI assistant

🍽️ How do I complete a meal?

Go to Meal Planner → Click "Mark as Completed (+10 pts)" on any meal → You'll earn 10 points instantly!

🎁 How are points calculated?

Each completed meal = 10 points. Complete all 3 meals daily for bonus points! Points can be redeemed in the Shopping section.

🤖 How does the AI meal plan work?

Our AI generates personalized meals based on your health conditions, dietary preferences, and calorie needs. Each meal is medically appropriate for your profile.

🔄 Can I change my meal plan?

Yes! Go to Meal Planner → Click "Regenerate Meal Plan" to create a new personalized plan.

⚖️ How do I update my weight?

Go to Profile → Update your weight in the form → Click Save Changes.

💧 How do I track water intake?

On the Meal Planner page, use the water tracker card to add 250ml, 500ml, or 1L at a time.

🛒 How do I redeem reward points?

Go to Shopping → Choose a reward → Click Redeem → Enjoy your discount!

📊 What does the Progress page show?

View your total meals, points, rank, weekly charts, and health scores all in one place!
